Before you review

This is a working workflow prototype. Actions use fictional records in your browser. They do not update staff, customer records, stock, bank payments or the live database.

Still separate: production integration, secure permissions, real document/photo storage, actual signatures and invoice/payment records, and deployment. Each trial has separate demo stock; shared live stock is not connected.

On hold: warranty / HQ API / credits, commission rules, cancellation charges and unresolved cash reconciliation rules.

Building planner: rooms can be moved and resized in 2D, with a matching 3D view. The layout is approximate; glass measurements remain separate. Complex wall shapes and drawing imports remain unfinished. Site presence is simulated; native GPS tracking is not connected.
